## 🎓 More Details (Optional Reading)

### What's Actually Happening?

Your database has two parts:
1. **Staff Records** - The actual staff data (name, photo, specialization, etc.)
2. **Vendor Lists** - Which staff belong to which vendor

Sometimes these get out of sync. This tool syncs them back up.

Think of it like:
- **Staff Records** = Your employees' HR files (they exist)
- **Vendor Lists** = The schedule board showing who works today (sometimes incomplete)
- **This Tool** = Automatically updates the schedule board to show everyone

## 📞 Still Need Help?

If clicking "Fix Now" doesn't solve your problem, check these:

### 1. Is the staff member actually created?
   - Go to Vendor App → Staff Management
   - Check if staff appears in the list

### 2. Does staff have specializations?
   - Staff must have at least one specialization
   - Check staff profile → Specializations section

### 3. Are services enabled?
   - Staff must have at least one ACTIVE service
   - Check staff profile → Services → Toggle ON

### 4. Is vendor approved?
   - Only approved vendors appear in searches
   - Check vendor status in Admin Portal
