# Part A — Onboarding & Setup

## 1. Vendor onboarding (sign-up to approved)

**Applies to:** Common (steps are the same; only **Step 2** and **Step 3** differ by role).

**What it is:** The path from first sign-in to “Approved”. You cannot use the dashboard or take bookings until you are approved.

**Where:** You start from the vendor app / web login. The app guides you through each step in order.

---

### Step-by-step

| Step | What you see on screen | What you must do |
|------|------------------------|------------------|
| **1. Sign in** | A screen asking for phone number | Enter your **mobile number**. Tap **Send OTP** or **Continue**. Check SMS for a 4–6 digit code. Enter that code and submit. |
| **2. Role selection** | A list of professions / business types | **Vet Solo:** Choose **Vet** or **Veterinarian**. **Vet Clinic:** Choose **Veterinary Clinic** or **Vet Clinic**. Do not pick another role. |
| **3. Vendor type** | “Solo Provider” vs “Business / Center” | **Vet Solo:** Choose **Solo Provider**. **Vet Clinic:** Choose **Business / Center**. The app may pre-select one; confirm it is correct. |
| **4. Application form** | One form with several fields | Fill **every required field**: name (business/display and/or contact), **address**, phone, email, and any extra fields (e.g. registration, GST). Then tap **Submit** or **Submit application**. |
| **5. Under review** | A message like “Your application is under review” | Do nothing except wait. If the platform asks for **clarification**, reply in the same place (e.g. upload a doc or add text). |
| **6. Approved** | A message like “You are approved” and a link or button to continue | Tap **Continue** or **Go to setup**. You will now complete **profile**, **bank**, **availability**, and **services** (see sections 3, 4, 6, 8) before you can go live. |
| **7. Activated** | Dashboard with menu (Bookings, Profile, Schedule, etc.) | This appears **after** you finish the go-live checklist and tap **Go Live** (see section 9). Only then can customers book you. |

**What each option means (Step 2–3):**

- **Vet / Veterinarian:** You are an individual vet. You get prescriptions, tele, at-home; no “at center” and no staff management.
- **Veterinary Clinic / Vet Clinic:** You are a clinic (business). You get at center, at home, tele, and can create packages.
- **Solo Provider:** One person; no staff or clinic listing in the app.
- **Business / Center:** Organization with a physical location; can offer in-clinic (at center) and packages.

**Solo vs Clinic:** Only the **choices** in Step 2 and Step 3 differ. All other steps (1, 4, 5, 6, 7) are the same.

## 2. After approval: go-live setup

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** After “Approved”, you must complete **four things** before the **Go Live** button works. Until then, customers cannot book you.

**Where:** The dashboard or a **Go-Live Checklist** screen shows these four items and whether each is done.

---

### Step-by-step

1. **Complete your business profile**  
   Go to **Profile** (see section 3). Fill in required and optional fields until the checklist shows “Profile complete” or at least **80%** completion.

2. **Add and verify a bank account**  
   Go to **Finance** → **Bank Account** (see section 4). Add one bank account and run **Verify**. It must show **Verified** for go-live.

3. **Add at least one service**  
   Go to **Services** (see section 6). Add a service, **enable** it, and **publish** it so customers can see and book it.

4. **Set your availability**  
   Go to **Schedule** (see section 8). Add at least **one day** with at least **one time slot** and tap **Save All**.

When all four show as complete, the **Go Live** button is enabled. Tap it to become bookable.

**What each item means:**  
They are the four blocks on the go-live checklist. No optional “fifth” step; staff management is not in the app.

## 3. Profile

**Applies to:** Common. **Vet Clinic only:** extra “Facility” part if the app shows it.

**What it is:** Your business/vendor profile. Customers and the platform use it for discovery and trust. You need about **80% completion** for go-live.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Profile** (or **Settings** → Profile). **Vet Clinic:** also **Facility** or **Settings** → Facility if available.

---

### Step-by-step

1. Open **Profile** from the dashboard.
2. Fill in these fields (required for go-live in practice):
   - **Business / Display name** — Name shown to customers (e.g. “Dr. X – Home Vet” or “Happy Paws Clinic”).
   - **Owner / Contact name** — Your full name or the person responsible for the account.
   - **Phone** — Usually pre-filled from sign-up; correct it if wrong.
   - **Email** — Used for notifications and support.
   - **Address** — Full address. For **Solo:** base/practice address. For **Clinic:** clinic address where customers come.
   - **Description / About** — Short text about what you offer (e.g. home visits, tele, specializations).
3. **Profile photo / logo** — Optional but recommended. Upload if the screen has an upload button.
4. Tap **Save** or **Update**. Check the go-live checklist; “Complete your business profile” should turn green when you are at or above 80%.

**Vet Clinic only — Facility (if you see it):**

- Open **Facility** (or equivalent) from the menu.
- You may see **Amenities** (e.g. parking, waiting area), **Operating hours** (can match your schedule), and **Policies** (e.g. cancellation). Fill what you need and save.

**What each field means:**

- **Business / Display name** = What appears in search and on the booking screen.
- **80% completion** = The system counts required and optional fields; you need a high score to pass the go-live “profile” item.

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same profile fields. Clinic has a physical **address** for “at center”; Solo uses **address** for “at home” area. Clinic may have an extra **Facility** section.

## 4. Bank account & verification

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** The bank account where you receive payouts. Only a **verified** account can receive money. Go-live requires at least one verified account.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Finance** → **Bank Account** (or **Settings** → Payment / Bank).

---

### Step-by-step

1. Open **Finance** → **Bank Account**.
2. Tap **Add bank account** (or **Add**).
3. Enter:
   - **Account holder name** — Exactly as in bank records (person or clinic name).
   - **Account number** — Full number, no spaces.
   - **IFSC code** — Branch IFSC (from cheque or net banking).
   - **Bank name** (and **branch** if asked).
4. Tap **Save** or **Save & Verify** (or **Update & Verify** if editing).
5. Tap **Verify** (if it’s a separate button). The app checks your details with the bank.
6. Check the result:
   - **Verified** — This account can receive payouts. Go-live “bank” item can be marked complete.
   - **Failed** — Correct name, IFSC, or account number and try **Verify** again.

**What each field means:**

- **Account holder name** — Must match the bank’s records; typos cause verification to fail.
- **IFSC** — 11-character code for the branch (e.g. from cheque).
- **Verified** — Platform has confirmed the account; only verified accounts are used for settlements.

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same steps and rules. Clinic may use a business account name; Solo usually a personal account name.

## 5. Tier system & commission

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** The platform uses **tiers** (e.g. Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum). Each tier has a **commission rate** (platform share of each booking) and **payout period** (how often you get paid). You see your tier on the dashboard or in Finance.

**Where:** Dashboard (tier badge) or **Finance** / **Tier** (if in menu).

---

### Step-by-step (understanding only; no setup by you)

1. Open the dashboard or **Finance**.
2. Note your **current tier** (e.g. Bronze).
3. If the app has a **Tier** or **Commission** screen, open it to see **commission rate** and **settlement breakup** (booking amount, platform share, your share).

**What each term means:**

- **Commission rate** — Percentage of each booking that the platform keeps (e.g. 15%).
- **Payout period** — How often settlements run (e.g. every 7 days).
- **Settlement breakup** — For a booking: total amount, commission, and your net payout.

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same; earnings are at vendor (solo or clinic) level.

# Part B — Capabilities (same format)

## 6. Managing services

**Applies to:** Common. **Vet Solo:** only **At home** and **Tele**. **Vet Clinic:** **At center**, **At home**, and **Tele**.

**What it is:** A **service** is one bookable offering (e.g. “General Consultation”, “Vaccination”, “Tele Consult”). You add services, set price and duration, choose **service style(s)**, then **enable** and **publish** so customers can book.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Services** (or **Manage services**).

---

### Step-by-step

1. Open **Services** from the dashboard.
2. Tap **Add service** or **Browse catalog** (if the app has a catalog).
3. **If from catalog:** Pick a template → set **price** and **duration** → choose **service style(s)** (see below) → **Enable** → **Publish**.
4. **If custom:**  
   - Enter **name** (e.g. “General Consultation”).  
   - Enter **description** (optional).  
   - Set **duration** in minutes (e.g. 30).  
   - Set **price**.  
   - Select **service style(s)**: **Vet Solo:** At home and/or Tele. **Vet Clinic:** At center, At home, and/or Tele.  
   - Tap **Save** or **Create**.
5. **Enable** the service (toggle or button) so it is active for you.
6. **Publish** the service (toggle or button) so it appears in customer search and booking. Unpublished = not bookable.

You can **edit** (price, duration, description) or **disable** later. Disabled or unpublished services are not bookable.

**What each option means:**

| Option | Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| **At center** | Customer comes to your clinic. **Vet Clinic only.** |
| **At home** | You (or your team) go to the customer’s address. |
| **Tele** | Video consultation; no physical visit. |
| **Enable** | Service is “on” for your account. |
| **Publish** | Service is visible to customers and can be booked. |

**Solo vs Clinic:** Solo cannot select **At center**. Clinic can use all three styles. Rest of the flow is the same.

## 7. Packages

**Applies to:** **Vet Clinic only** (business). **Vet Solo:** platform does not allow creating packages; skip this section.

**What it is:** A **package** is a bundle (e.g. “5 consultations”) that a customer buys once and then uses for bookings. Only **business** (clinic) accounts can create packages.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Services** → **Packages** (or **Packages** in the menu).

---

### Step-by-step (Vet Clinic only)

1. Open **Packages** from the dashboard.
2. Tap **Create package**.
3. Enter:
   - **Package name** (e.g. “5 General Consultations”).
   - **Package type** — e.g. Session, Combo, Subscription, or Unlimited (use the dropdown in the app).
   - **Description**, **Original price** (optional), **Package price**.
   - **Validity** — e.g. 30, 60, 90 days.
   - **Usage** — Total sessions (e.g. 5) or **Unlimited**.
   - **Included services** — Which of your services can be used from this package.
   - Any **Benefits** or **Terms** if the form has them.
4. Tap **Save** or **Create**. The package then appears for customers when they search or book.

**What each option means:**

- **Session** — Fixed number of sessions in a validity period.  
- **Combo** — Bundle of different services at one price.  
- **Subscription / Membership** — Recurring or membership-style (if supported).  
- **Unlimited** — Unlimited sessions in a period (e.g. monthly).

## 8. Availability (Advanced Availability)

**Applies to:** Common. Same screen for both; **Vet Solo** cannot add **At center** slots and has a **Go Offline** toggle. **Vet Clinic** can add At center.

**What it is:** **Advanced Availability** is where you set your weekly schedule: for each day you add **time slots**, optionally **breaks** and **holidays**, then **Save All**. Customers only see bookable slots within these windows.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Schedule**. This opens the Advanced Availability screen.

---

### Step-by-step (same for both; differences called out)

**Step 1 — Open Schedule**

1. From the dashboard, open **Schedule** (under Operations or main menu).
2. You should see:
   - **Day tabs:** Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat.
   - **Vet Solo only:** A toggle like **You are Online** / **Go Offline** at the top. When **Offline**, customers cannot see or book you.
   - Below: list of **time slots** for the selected day, and sections for **Breaks** and **Holidays**.

**Step 2 — Add time slots for each working day**

1. Click a **day tab** (e.g. Monday).
2. Tap **Add slot** (or similar). A new slot appears (e.g. 09:00–17:00).
3. Set **Start time** and **End time** (e.g. 09:00–13:00 and 14:00–18:00). Do **not** let two slots on the same day overlap; the app will warn you.
4. Set **service style(s)** for this slot:
   - **Vet Solo:** Tick **At home** and/or **Tele** (no At center).
   - **Vet Clinic:** Tick **At center**, **At home**, and/or **Tele** as needed.
5. **Lead time (optional):** If you see **Travel time (min)** (at home), **Prep time (min)** (at center), or **Setup time (min)** (tele), set them so the system doesn’t book back-to-back without buffer.
6. Ensure the slot is **enabled** (default is on).
7. Repeat for every working day. Use **Copy to all days** or **Copy to weekdays** to copy the current day’s slots, then adjust times if needed.

**Step 3 — Add breaks (optional)**

1. Expand **Breaks**.
2. Tap **Add break**. Set **day** (or recurring for selected day), **start time**, **end time**, and **type** (e.g. lunch, tea, personal). Do not overlap a slot; the app will warn you.
3. You can **Copy breaks to all days** or **Copy to weekdays**.

**Step 4 — Add holidays / vacation (optional)**

1. Expand **Holidays**.
2. Tap **Add holiday** (or Add vacation). Enter **start date**, **end date**, and optionally **type** (e.g. vacation, closed) and **reason**. On these dates no slots are offered.

**Step 5 — Save**

1. Tap **Save All** (top right).
2. The app saves slots, breaks, and holidays. You should see something like “Saved: X slots, Y breaks, Z holidays.”
3. If you get an **overlap** error, fix the overlapping slot or break on the day mentioned and save again.

**Step 6 — Go Offline (Vet Solo only)**

- Use the **You are Online / Offline** toggle when you don’t want new bookings (e.g. day off). **Offline** = hidden from discovery and not bookable.

**What each option means:**

| Option | Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| **Service style per slot** | Which booking types (at center / at home / tele) are available in that time window. |
| **Lead time** | Buffer so the system doesn’t book you in two places at once (e.g. travel time for at home). |
| **Break** | Time block when you are not available (e.g. lunch); no slots are shown in that range. |
| **Holiday** | Date range when you are closed; no slots on those dates. |

**Solo vs Clinic:** Solo has **Go Offline** and cannot add **At center** to any slot. Clinic can add At center. Rest is the same.

## 9. Going live

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** After the four go-live items (profile, bank, at least one service, availability) are complete, you tap **Go Live** once. After that you are discoverable and bookable.

**Where:** Go-Live Checklist screen or dashboard (same place that shows the four items).

---

### Step-by-step

1. Complete all four go-live items (sections 3, 4, 6, 8).
2. Open the **Go-Live Checklist** (or equivalent).
3. Confirm each of the four items shows as complete (e.g. green tick).
4. Tap **Go Live**. You should see a success message.
5. Your profile and published services are now visible to customers; they can book within your availability.

You can still change profile, bank, services, and availability after going live.

# Part C — Appointment lifecycle (by style)

## 10. When an appointment is received

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** When a customer books and pays (or uses a package), you get a new appointment in **Pending** state. You must **Accept** or **Reject**. After you accept, the appointment moves to **Confirmed** and you follow the lifecycle for that **style** (at center, at home, tele, or instant).

**Where:** Dashboard → **Bookings** (or **Appointments**). You may see **Today’s bookings** and **Pending**.

---

### Step-by-step

1. When a new booking arrives, you may get a **notification**. Open **Bookings**.
2. Find the appointment with status **Pending**.
3. Open it. You will see: customer name, pet, service, date/time, address (for at home) or clinic (at center), and any notes.
4. Choose one:
   - **Accept** — The booking becomes **Confirmed**. The customer is notified. You are expected to do the service at the scheduled time.
   - **Reject** — You may be asked for a reason. The booking is cancelled; the customer can book someone else.
5. After **Accept**, use section 11 to handle the appointment by its **style** (at center, at home, tele, or instant).

**What each status means:**

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| **Pending** | New; waiting for you to Accept or Reject. |
| **Confirmed** | You accepted; next step is Start (if applicable) or Join / Complete as per style. |
| **In progress** | Service has started (e.g. visit started or video call ongoing). |
| **Completed** | You completed the appointment (OTP or “Mark complete” as per style). |
| **Cancelled** | Rejected or cancelled. |

## 11. Appointment lifecycle by style (at center, at home, tele, instant)

**Applies to:** Common. **At center** is **Vet Clinic only**. **At home** and **Tele** and **Instant** are both.

This section is the **exact step-by-step** for each way customers book you. Use the table to see which steps apply, then follow the numbered steps for that style.

---

### Quick reference table

| Style | Who | Start (what you do) | Complete (what you do) |
|-------|-----|---------------------|------------------------|
| **At center** | Vet Clinic only | Optional check-in; no OTP to start | Ask customer for **completion OTP** → enter in app → tap **Complete** |
| **At home** | Both | Optional **Start Travel** (no OTP). No “Start Session” OTP for vet | Ask customer for **completion OTP** → enter in app → tap **Complete** |
| **Tele** | Both | Join the **video call** at booked time | After call, tap **Complete** or **Mark complete** (no OTP) |
| **Instant** | Both | Join the **video call** when notified | After call, tap **Complete** or **Mark complete** (no OTP) |

---

### At center (Vet Clinic only)

**When:** Customer comes to your clinic at the booked time.

1. **Before the slot:** Ensure the appointment shows as **Confirmed** in **Bookings**.
2. **When the customer arrives:** You may have an optional **Check-in** or **Start** (no OTP required to start).
3. **After the consultation:** Ask the customer for the **completion OTP** they received (SMS/app). Open the appointment → enter the OTP → tap **Complete**. Status becomes **Completed**; earnings and settlement flow run.
4. **Optional:** Add a prescription or consultation summary from the appointment (see section 14).

---

### At home (both Solo and Clinic)

**When:** You (or your team) go to the customer’s address.

1. **Before the visit:** Note the **address** and **time** from the appointment. You may see **Start Travel** — tap it if you want the customer to see your live location; **no OTP is required** to start the visit.
2. **At the customer’s location:** Do the consultation. There is **no “Start Session” OTP** for vet at-home (that exists only for roles like dog walking).
3. **After the visit:** Ask the customer for the **completion OTP** (SMS/app). Open the appointment → enter the OTP → tap **Complete**. Status becomes **Completed**; earnings and settlement flow run.
4. **Optional:** Add a prescription or consultation summary (see section 14).

---

### Tele (scheduled video) (both)

**When:** Video consultation at the booked time.

1. **At the booked time:** Open the appointment and tap **Join video call** or **Video call** (or use the link in Chat). Join the call; the customer joins from their side.
2. **During the call:** Conduct the consultation. You can use in-call chat if available.
3. **After the call:** End the call from the app. Then open the appointment and tap **Complete** or **Mark complete**. **No OTP** for tele. Status becomes **Completed**; earnings and settlement flow run.
4. **Optional:** Add a prescription or consultation summary (see section 14).

---

### Instant (video, no fixed slot) (both)

**When:** Customer chose “Instant” and was connected to an available vet. You get a notification to join.

1. **When you get the notification:** Tap to **Join** the video call. Join as soon as you can.
2. **During the call:** Same as Tele — conduct the consultation.
3. **After the call:** End the call, then open the appointment and tap **Complete** or **Mark complete**. No OTP. Add prescription/summary if needed.

---

**What each action means:**

- **Start Travel** — Shares your location with the customer; no OTP. Optional.
- **Completion OTP** — Only for **at center** and **at home**. Customer gets it by SMS/app; you enter it to prove the service was done.
- **Mark complete** — For **tele** and **instant**; no OTP. You confirm the consultation is over.

**Solo vs Clinic:** At center is Clinic only. At home, tele, and instant are the same for both.

---

## 12. Chat & video with customer

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** For any appointment you can open **Chat** to send messages (and sometimes files). For **tele** and **instant** you use **Video call** from the same appointment or chat.

**Where:** Inside the appointment detail → **Chat** or **Messages**; **Video call** or **Join call** for tele/instant.

---

### Step-by-step — Chat

1. Open the appointment from **Bookings**.
2. Tap **Chat** or **Messages**. Type your message and send. Use it to confirm address, time, or to ask for the **completion OTP** when finishing an at-home or at-center visit. Do not ask for card or bank details.
3. You can send follow-up instructions or prescription notes in addition to the formal prescription (section 14).

### Step-by-step — Video (tele / instant)

1. From the appointment (or chat), tap **Video call** or **Join call** at the scheduled time (or when the instant call is assigned).
2. Allow camera and microphone if the browser/app asks. Join the call.
3. Use in-call controls (mute, camera on/off, end call). You may have in-call chat.
4. When done, tap **End call**. **Ending the call does not complete the appointment** — you must tap **Complete** or **Mark complete** on the appointment (section 13).

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same for both.

---

## 13. Completing an appointment

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** “Complete” is the last step of the appointment lifecycle. It closes the visit, triggers earnings, and (for paid bookings) queues settlement to your bank. How you complete depends on the **style** (see section 11).

**Where:** Same appointment detail screen where you see Accept/Reject, Start Travel, Video call, etc. The **Complete** or **Mark complete** button is there (or in an OTP modal for at center / at home).

---

### Step-by-step (by style)

- **At center:** Enter the **completion OTP** from the customer → tap **Complete**.
- **At home:** Enter the **completion OTP** from the customer → tap **Complete**.
- **Tele / Instant:** No OTP. After the video call, tap **Complete** or **Mark complete** on the appointment.

After **Completed** and when payment is **paid** (or package/subscription used), the platform creates **vendor_earnings** and queues **settlement** to your verified bank (see section 15). You can still open the appointment later to add or edit a prescription or consultation summary (section 14).

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same rules; only at center is Clinic only.

---

## 14. Prescribing & consultation summary

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** After (or during) a consultation you can add a **prescription** (medications, diagnosis, instructions) and/or a **consultation summary** (vet summary). You can save as **draft** (only you see) or **publish** (customer sees; may be used for pharmacy orders).

**Where:** From the **appointment detail** → **Prescription** or **Add prescription** / **Add consultation summary**.

---

### Step-by-step — Prescription

1. Open the **completed** (or current) appointment.
2. Tap **Prescription** or **Add prescription**. Booking and pet are usually pre-filled.
3. Add **medications:** name, dosage, frequency, duration, instructions. Add **diagnosis** and **general instructions** (e.g. diet, rest). Optionally **follow-up date** or **follow-up notes**.
4. Choose **Save as draft** (edit later) or **Publish**. **Publish** makes it part of the customer’s medical records and may be shared in chat or used for pharmacy orders.

### Step-by-step — Consultation summary (vet summary)

1. From the appointment, tap **Add consultation summary** or **Vet summary**.
2. Enter **diagnosis**, **notes**, **vitals** (e.g. weight, temperature), **symptoms**, and **medications**.
3. Choose **Save as draft** or **Publish**. Published summary is shared (e.g. in chat) and stored in history.

**What each option means:**

- **Draft** — Only you see it; you can edit and publish later.
- **Publish** — Customer sees it; it is stored and may be used for ordering medicine (if the app supports pharmacy).

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same for both.

# Part D — Earnings & settings

## 15. Earnings & settlements

**Applies to:** Common.

**What it is:** After each **completed**, **paid** booking, your share (booking amount minus platform commission) is recorded as **earnings** and added to **pending payout**. Settlements are batched (e.g. weekly) and transferred to your **verified** bank account.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Finance** → **Earnings** and **Settlements** (or equivalent).

---

### Step-by-step (understanding and checking only)

1. Open **Finance** → **Earnings** to see per-booking breakdown: amount, commission, your net.
2. Check **Pending payout** (total not yet transferred) and **Settlements** (batched payouts: pending → processing → completed or failed).
3. Payouts go to your **verified** bank account (section 4). If a settlement fails, fix the bank details, re-verify, and contact support if needed.

**What each term means:**

- **Earnings** — Your share per booking after commission.
- **Pending payout** — Sum of earnings not yet sent to your bank.
- **Settlements** — Batches of payouts; status can be pending, processing, completed, or failed.

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same; earnings are at vendor level.

## 16. Settings & day-to-day

**Applies to:** Common. **Vet Clinic only:** Facility (if available).

**What it is:** Day-to-day changes: update profile, bank, schedule, services; use vacation/holidays; keep notifications on.

**Where:** **Profile**, **Finance** → **Bank Account**, **Schedule**, **Services**, and **Settings** (or notifications) from the dashboard.

---

### Step-by-step (what you can do anytime)

1. **Profile** — Update name, contact, address, description, photo from **Profile** or **Settings**.
2. **Bank** — Add or change bank accounts under **Finance** → **Bank Account**. Always **verify** after changes so payouts continue.
3. **Schedule** — Change availability (slots, breaks, holidays) in **Schedule** and tap **Save All**. **Vet Solo:** Use **Go Offline** when not taking bookings.
4. **Services** — Add, edit, enable/disable, or publish/unpublish services under **Services**. New services are bookable only when published and within your availability.
5. **Notifications** — Keep app notifications on so you see new bookings and messages quickly.
6. **Support** — For verification failures, commission, or payout issues, use in-app support or Warmpawz contact details.

**Solo vs Clinic:** Same; Clinic may have **Facility** in Settings/Operations.
