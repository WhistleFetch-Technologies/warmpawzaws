## 1. Onboarding: From Sign-Up to Approved

As a **solo vet**, you are an individual practitioner (no clinic or staff). The platform guides you through these steps in order.

| Step | What you see | What to do |
|------|----------------|------------|
| **1. Sign in** | Enter phone number | Enter your mobile number. You will receive a one-time code (OTP) by SMS. Enter the OTP to verify. |
| **2. Role selection** | List of professions | Choose **Vet** (or **Veterinarian**). This sets your capabilities (prescriptions, tele, etc.). |
| **3. Vendor type** | Solo vs Business | Choose **Solo Provider**. For vets working alone (home visits, tele), this is the correct option. The app may auto-select it. |
| **4. Application form** | Single dynamic form | Fill in: business/contact name, address, phone, email, and any role-specific or KYC fields. Complete all required fields and submit. |
| **5. Under review** | “Under review” screen | Wait for Warmpawz to review your application. You may be asked for clarification; respond via the same flow. |
| **6. Approved** | “Approved” screen | Once approved, you will be directed to complete **profile**, **bank**, **schedule**, and **services** before you can go live. |
| **7. Activated** | Dashboard | After you complete the go-live checklist and go live, you get full access to the vendor dashboard and can receive bookings. |

**Important:** Do not skip steps. The system will not let you access the dashboard or take bookings until you are **approved** and have completed the **go-live checklist**.

## 2. After Approval: Go-Live Setup

After approval, you must complete these **four required items** before you can go live:

1. **Complete your business profile** (see [§3](#3-profile-configuration)) — at least 80% completion (name, contact, address, etc.).
2. **Add and verify a bank account** (see [§4](#4-bank-account--verification)) — required for receiving payouts.
3. **Add at least one service** (see [§6](#6-services-what-they-are--how-to-configure)) — and enable/publish it so customers can book.
4. **Set your availability schedule** (see [§8](#8-setting-your-availability)) — at least one day with time windows.

The dashboard or **Go-Live Checklist** screen shows each item as complete or pending. When all four are complete, the **Go Live** button becomes available. Only then will you appear as bookable to customers.

## 3. Profile Configuration

**Where:** Dashboard → **Profile** (or **Settings** → Profile).

**What to fill in:**

- **Business / Display name** — How you appear to customers (e.g. “Dr. [Name] – Home Vet”).
- **Owner/Contact name** — Your full name.
- **Phone** — Already set from sign-up; ensure it’s correct.
- **Email** — For official communication and notifications.
- **Address** — Your practice or base address (used for “at home” service area and discovery).
- **Description / About** — Short description of your practice (e.g. home visits, tele-consultation, specializations).
- **Profile photo** — Optional but recommended for trust.

**What “profile completion” means:** The system calculates a percentage from required and optional fields. You need **at least 80%** to satisfy the go-live “Complete your business profile” requirement.

**Solo vet limits:** You do **not** have “Staff” or “Clinic” management. You offer only **At home** and **Tele** (video) — no “at center” (that’s for vet clinics).

## 4. Bank Account & Verification

**Why it matters:** Payouts (settlements) are sent only to a **verified** bank account. Until your account is verified, you cannot receive earnings.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Finance** → **Bank Account** (or **Settings** → Payment / Bank).

**Steps:**

1. **Add bank account**  
   Enter:
   - **Account holder name** — Exactly as on the bank record.
   - **Account number** — Full account number (no spaces).
   - **IFSC code** — Bank branch IFSC (e.g. from cheque or net banking).
   - **Bank name** (and branch if asked).

2. **Save**  
   The form may offer **Save & Verify** or **Update & Verify**.

3. **Verify**  
   The platform validates your account with the bank (e.g. name + IFSC + account number).  
   - If verification **succeeds**: the account is marked **Verified** and can be used for payouts.  
   - If it **fails**: correct the details (typos, wrong IFSC/name) and try again. Only verified accounts receive settlements.

**Multiple accounts:** You can add more than one account; one can be set as **primary** for payouts. Only **verified** accounts are used for transfers.

## 5. Tier System & Commission

**What it is:** Warmpawz uses **tiers** (e.g. Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum). Each tier has:

- **Commission rate** — Percentage of each booking that the platform keeps (e.g. 15% for Bronze, lower for higher tiers).
- **Payout period** — How often settlements are run (e.g. every 7 days).
- **Monthly/yearly cost** — Paid tiers may have a subscription fee; free tier (e.g. Bronze) has no fee but a higher commission.

**Where you see it:** Dashboard may show your current tier (e.g. “Bronze Tier”). Finance or tier screens may show:

- Your **commission rate**.
- **Settlement breakup** — total booking amount, platform commission, and your net payout.

**Upgrading:** If your vendor type supports tier upgrades, you may see options to upgrade (e.g. pay monthly/yearly or via deduction from future settlements). Check the **Finance** or **Tier** section in the app for exact options.

**For solo vets:** You are subject to the same tier and commission rules as other vendors; your earnings after each completed, paid booking are: **booking amount − platform commission**.

## 6. Services: What They Are & How to Configure

**What a “service” is:** A bookable offering — e.g. “General Consultation”, “Vaccination”, “Follow-up Tele Consult”. Each service has:

- **Name**
- **Description** (optional)
- **Duration** (minutes)
- **Price**
- **Service style:** for solo vet only **At home** and **Tele** are allowed.

**Service styles (solo vet):**

| Style   | Meaning |
|--------|--------|
| **At home** | You go to the customer’s location. Customer may need to enter address; OTP is used to start/complete. |
| **Tele**    | Video consultation. Customer books a slot; you and the customer join a video call; no OTP for completion. |

You can have different services for **at home** and **tele** (e.g. “Home Check-up”, “Video Consultation”), each with its own price and duration.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Services** (or **Manage services**).

**How to add a service:**

1. Open **Services** and choose **Add service** or **Browse catalog** (if the platform provides a catalog).
2. **From catalog:** Pick a service template, set **price** and **duration**, and **enable** it for your vendor.
3. **Custom service:** Create a new service: name, description, duration, price, and select **At home** and/or **Tele**.
4. **Enable** the service so it appears in your list of active offerings.
5. **Publish** (if the app has a publish step) so it becomes visible to customers in search and booking.

**Enable vs Publish:**

- **Enable** — Service is active for you and can be attached to slots.
- **Publish** — Service is visible to customers in discovery and booking. Until published, customers cannot book it.

You can **edit** (price, duration, description) or **disable** services anytime. Disabled or unpublished services are not bookable.

## 7. Packages (Optional)

**What packages are:** Bundles of sessions or benefits (e.g. “5 tele consultations”, “3 home visits”). Customers buy the package once and use sessions against it when booking.

**Solo vet restriction (from platform rules):** Solo vendors **cannot** create packages in the standard way. Package creation is typically allowed for **business** accounts (e.g. clinics) or for specific solo roles like trainers/walkers/sitters/groomers (session packages only). If you do not see **Packages** or **Create package** in your menu, this is expected for solo vets. If you need packages, you may need to contact support or consider a business (clinic) account.

**If your account allows packages:** You would define package name, type (e.g. session), price, validity (days), number of sessions (or unlimited), and which services are included. For exact options, use the **Packages** screen under **Services** if it is available to you.

## 8. Setting Your Availability (Advanced Availability)

The platform uses **Advanced Availability**: weekly schedule with multiple slots per day, breaks, and holidays. Follow these steps exactly.

**Where:** Dashboard → **Schedule** (opens the Advanced Availability screen).

---

### Step 1: Open Schedule

1. From the dashboard, go to **Schedule** (under Operations or main menu).
2. The **Advanced Availability** screen loads. You will see:
   - **Day tabs** at the top: Sun, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat.
   - For **solo providers**: a **Go Offline** / **You are Online** toggle at the top (when offline, customers cannot see or book you).
   - Below: the list of **time slots** for the selected day, plus **Breaks** and **Holidays** sections.

---

### Step 2: Add Time Slots for Each Working Day

1. **Select a day** by clicking its tab (e.g. Monday).
2. Click **Add slot** (or equivalent). A new slot appears with default times (e.g. 09:00–17:00).
3. **Set the time window:**
   - **Start time** and **End time** — e.g. 09:00 to 13:00, then add another slot 14:00 to 18:00.
   - **Do not overlap** two slots on the same day; the app will warn you. Adjust times so slots do not overlap each other or any break.
4. **Set service styles for this slot:** For solo vet you can select **At home** and/or **Tele** (no “At center”). Tick the styles you offer in this window (e.g. both At home and Tele).
5. **Lead time (optional):** You may see **Travel time (min)** for At home and **Setup time (min)** for Tele. These are used so the system does not book back-to-back appointments without buffer (e.g. 45 min travel for at home, 5 min for tele).
6. **Enable the slot** — ensure the slot is enabled (default is on).
7. Repeat for **every day you work**. You can use **Copy to all days** or **Copy to weekdays** to copy the current day’s slots to other days, then edit as needed.

**Solo vet:** Only **At home** and **Tele** appear; “At center” is not available for solo.

---

### Step 3: Add Breaks (Optional)

1. Expand the **Breaks** section.
2. Click **Add break**.
3. Choose **day** (or “recurring” for the selected day every week), **start time**, **end time**, and **type** (e.g. lunch, tea, personal).
4. Ensure the break **does not overlap** any time slot on that day; the app will warn you if it does.
5. You can **Copy breaks to all days** or **Copy to weekdays** to reuse the same break pattern.

---

### Step 4: Add Holidays / Vacation (Optional)

1. Expand the **Holidays** section.
2. Click **Add holiday** (or Add vacation).
3. Enter **start date** and **end date**. Optionally set **type** (e.g. vacation, closed, personal) and **reason**.
4. On these dates, **no slots** are offered to customers.

---

### Step 5: Save

1. Click **Save All** (top right).
2. The app saves **slots**, **breaks**, and **holidays** in one go. You should see a success message (e.g. “Saved: X slots, Y breaks, Z holidays”).
3. If you see an error about **overlapping slots or breaks**, fix the overlapping time on the day mentioned, then save again.

---

### Step 6: Go Offline (Solo Only)

- If you are a **solo provider**, use the **You are Online / Offline** toggle when you do not want to receive new bookings (e.g. day off without adding a holiday). When **Offline**, you are hidden from discovery and no new slots are bookable.

---

**Go-live:** Until at least one day has at least one **saved** time slot, the go-live checklist item **“Set up your availability schedule”** will not be complete. Complete Step 1–5 and click **Save All**.

---

## 9. Going Live

When all four go-live items are done (profile ≥80%, bank verified, at least one service added and enabled/published, schedule set):

1. Open the **Go-Live Checklist** (or equivalent) from the dashboard.
2. Confirm all items show as complete.
3. Click **Go Live**.

After that, your profile and services become discoverable and customers can book you. You can still change profile, bank, services, and schedule after going live.

## 10. When an Appointment Is Received

**Where you see it:** Dashboard → **Bookings** (or **Appointments**). You may see **Today’s bookings** and **Pending** (awaiting your action).

**Status flow (typical):**

- **Pending** — New booking; customer has paid (or used a package). You must **Accept** or **Reject**.
- **Confirmed** — You accepted. Next step is to **Start** (for at home) or join the **Video call** (for tele).
- **In progress** — Session has started (e.g. you started the visit with OTP, or the video call is ongoing).
- **Completed** — You have completed the appointment (OTP completion for at home, or marking complete after tele/video).
- **Cancelled** — You or the customer cancelled, or you rejected.

**When a new booking arrives:**

1. You may get a **notification** (in-app or push).
2. Open **Bookings** and find the appointment with status **Pending**.
3. Open the appointment to see: customer name, pet, service, date/time, address (for at home), and any notes.
4. Choose:
   - **Accept** — Booking becomes **Confirmed**. Customer is notified. You are expected to perform the service at the scheduled time.
   - **Reject** — You may be asked to enter a reason. Booking is cancelled; customer can rebook with someone else.

Accept or reject in a timely manner so the customer knows whether the appointment is confirmed.

## 11. Handling Appointments by Type

### At home

- **Before the visit:** Note the address and time. You may see **Start Travel** (optional) — use it if you want the customer to see your live location while you travel; **no OTP is required to start** the visit.
- **At the customer’s location:** Conduct the visit. There is **no “Start Session” OTP** for at-home vet services (start OTP is only used for certain roles such as dog walking).
- **After the visit:** Ask the customer for the **completion OTP** they received (SMS/app). Enter it in the app and tap **Complete**. The appointment status becomes **Completed** and earnings are recorded. You can then add a prescription or summary if needed (see [§14](#14-prescribing--consultation-summary)).

### Tele (video consultation)

- **No OTP** to start or complete. You and the customer join the **video call** at the booked time (via the link/button in the appointment or in **Chat**).
- **During the call:** Conduct the consultation. You can use in-call chat if available.
- **After the call:** End the call from the app. Then **mark the appointment as completed** (e.g. “Complete” on the appointment detail). You can add a **prescription** or **consultation summary** (see [§14](#14-prescribing--consultation-summary)). Completion triggers earnings and settlement flow.

### Instant tele (if available for your role)

- Some flows offer **Instant** video consultation (customer pays and gets connected to an available vet without a fixed slot). If you are in the “available now” pool and accept or get assigned:
  - Join the video call when notified.
  - Same as scheduled tele: no OTP; complete the consultation and **mark completed** when done; add prescription/summary as needed.

**Summary:**

| Type      | Start                         | Complete                    |
|----------|--------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| At home  | Start Travel (optional, no OTP); no start OTP for vet | OTP → Complete              |
| Tele     | Join video call                | End call → Mark complete    |
| Instant  | Join video call                | End call → Mark complete    |

## 12. Customer Interaction: Chat & Video

**Chat:** From the appointment detail, open **Chat** (or **Messages**). You and the customer can send text messages and optionally images. Use it for:

- Confirming address or time.
- Asking for the **completion OTP** when finishing an at-home visit (do not ask for card or bank details).
- Sending follow-up instructions or prescription notes (in addition to the formal prescription).

**Video:** For **Tele** and **Instant** appointments, **Video call** is started from the same appointment or chat screen. Join at the scheduled time (or when the instant call is assigned). During the call you may have:

- Mute/unmute, camera on/off.
- In-call chat.
- End call button.

Ending the call does **not** automatically complete the appointment — you must **mark the appointment as completed** and, if applicable, add a prescription or consultation summary.

---

## 13. Completing an Appointment

- **At home:** Enter the **completion OTP** from the customer and tap **Complete**. Status → **Completed**.
- **Tele / Instant:** No OTP. After the video call, open the appointment and tap **Complete** (or “Mark as completed”). Status → **Completed**.

Once **Completed** and payment is **paid** (or package/subscription used), the platform:

- Creates a **vendor_earnings** record (your share after commission).
- Queues a **settlement** (payout to your verified bank account per the tier’s payout schedule).

You can still open the appointment later to add or edit **prescription** or **consultation summary** if you did not do it at completion.

## 14. Prescribing & Consultation Summary

**Who it’s for:** Vets (and optionally nutritionists). As a solo vet you have access to **Prescriptions** and **Vet Summary** (consultation summary).

**When:** After (or during) a consultation — for the same booking. The appointment must exist and ideally be **Completed** so the prescription is linked to the right visit.

**Where:** From the **appointment detail** → **Prescription** or **Add prescription** / **Add consultation summary**.

**Prescription:**

- **Booking** and **Pet** are usually pre-filled.
- Add **medications:** name, dosage, frequency, duration, instructions.
- **Diagnosis** and **General instructions** (e.g. diet, rest).
- **Follow-up date** or **Follow-up notes** (optional).
- **Save as draft** — Only you see it; you can edit and **Publish** later.
- **Publish** — Becomes part of the customer’s medical records and may be shared in chat or visible to the customer in the app. They can use it to order medicine (e.g. pharmacy flow) if the platform supports it.

**Consultation summary (Vet summary):**

- **Diagnosis**, **Notes**, **Vitals** (e.g. weight, temperature), **Symptoms**.
- **Medications** (same idea as prescription).
- **Save as draft** or **Publish**. Published summary is shared (e.g. in chat) and stored in history.

**Best practice:** Publish the prescription (or summary) after the consultation so the customer has a clear record and can follow up or order medicines.

## 15. Settlements & Earnings

**Earnings:** After each **completed**, **paid** booking, your share (booking amount − platform commission) is recorded as **earnings** and added to **pending payout**.

**Where to see it:** Dashboard → **Finance** → **Earnings** (and possibly **Settlements**).

- **Earnings** — Per-booking breakdown: amount, commission, your net.
- **Pending payout** — Sum not yet transferred to your bank.
- **Settlements** — Batched payouts (e.g. weekly). Each settlement has a status: pending → processing → completed (or failed).

**When do you get paid:** According to your **tier** (e.g. every 7 days). The system (or admin) runs settlements and transfers money to your **verified** bank account. If a settlement fails (e.g. wrong account), you will need to fix the bank details and re-verify; support can help.

**Bank verification:** Only **verified** accounts receive payouts. Keep your bank details correct and verified (see [§4](#4-bank-account--verification)).

## 16. Settings & Day-to-Day

**Profile:** Update name, contact, address, photo, description anytime from **Profile** or **Settings**.

**Bank:** Add or update bank accounts under **Finance** → **Bank Account**. Always **verify** after changes so payouts can continue.

**Schedule:** Change availability (days, times, slot duration, service style) under **Schedule**. Save after edits. Use **Vacation** or **Time off** if available to block dates.

**Services:** Add, edit, enable/disable, or publish/unpublish services under **Services**. New services will be bookable once published and within your availability.

**Notifications:** Keep app notifications on so you see new bookings and messages promptly.

**Support:** For technical or policy issues (e.g. verification failures, wrong commission, missing payout), use in-app support or the contact details provided by Warmpawz.
